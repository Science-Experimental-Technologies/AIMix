# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The AIMix community is committed to a professional, welcoming, and harassment-free environment. Participate with empathy, technical honesty, patience, and respect for different backgrounds and experience levels.

Acceptable participation includes constructive feedback, clear disagreement focused on ideas, responsible security disclosure, and acknowledgement of mistakes. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's credentials or private data are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people, the project, or its users. Reports should be sent privately through the official repository's moderation or security channel. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
